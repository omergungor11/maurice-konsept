Over the Moon

Client: Netflix Akcelo Sydney

Bringing bunny rockets to the streets of Bangkok

To launch Netflix's new animated movie Over the Moon, we took our protagonist Fei Fei's iconic bunny rocket and created a fleet of tuk tuk's which delivered passengers to our pièce de résistance; the giant inflatable moon from the movie complete with fireworks and laser light show. What a hoot.
