In Loving Memory

Client: Supercheap Auto (spec) Unmade · Black Friday film, in-store, social

Black Friday prices. Our profits didn’t make it.

Every Black Friday ad says the prices are so low it hurts. This one shows who it hurts. The finance team drive down from head office in their good suits and hold a service in aisle four, for the till. There are lilies. There’s a eulogy, and the man giving it doesn’t get far. Then one of them goes for the jumper leads.

Nobody on the shop floor has time for it. The staff are flat out, stepping around grieving accountants to get to the next customer, and the customers are walking out with the deals. (On social, head office keeps posting updates from the wake. They don’t get better.)
