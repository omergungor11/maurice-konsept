Spread Out

Client: Tourism Australia (spec) Unmade · Large-format outdoor, film

Come down and spread out.

Everyone knows what Venice looks like. Palazzo, palazzo, palazzo, shoulder to shoulder, because there was never any more land. So Tourism Australia shows the world’s most famously cramped places the way they’d have been built with Australia’s room. Same Venice. Same New York. Except every building gets a backyard, and the backyard gets used the way we use ours: cricket, a trampoline, washing on the line, something on the barbie. And there’s always a kangaroo about.

It runs as large-format outdoor in the cities it’s about, the wider the better. Nobody in the pictures thinks anything of it. That’s just how much room there is.

Come down and spread out.
